Leaving…
The quiet problem

People are leaving, and most never get a second chance

A lot of brands focus heavily on getting people through the door.

More adsMore trafficMore campaigns

But underneath all of that, visitors leave without giving the brand another chance to bring them back. That was the opportunity we saw with this ecommerce brand.

The problem wasn’t a lack of forms. It was that the forms were treated as individual tools, not parts of one customer journey.

A first-time visitor shouldn’t see the same experience as someone who has spent several minutes exploring products and is now moving the cursor toward the exit. So instead of adding another generic popup, we asked: what can we show someone at the exact moment they’re about to leave?

The strategy

We gave the exit-intent moment a job

The new form wasn’t designed to interrupt every visitor. It steps in only when someone is already showing signs of leaving, and it works with the existing forms instead of competing with them.

The results

And the numbers made the case

A 16% submit rate means roughly 1 in every 6 views became a submission. That doesn’t come from simply adding a popup. It comes from putting the right interaction in the right moment.

The bigger lesson

Exit intent shouldn’t mean one last desperate popup

What it usually is

“Wait! Don’t go! Here’s a popup.”

What it should be

“We’ve recognized that you’re leaving. Here’s one more reason to continue the conversation.”

That shift changes how you build the experience. We weren’t trying to force every visitor into a form. We were building a system that recognizes when a visitor is worth re-engaging and gives them a relevant next step.

We didn’t add another form. We added another opportunity to convert intent.
